Inside the project.

BVB 3D Stadium is an original architectural study inspired by Signal Iduna Park in Dortmund. It is not affiliated with Borussia Dortmund, the stadium operator, or a ticket seller.

Places, views, prices, and availability are illustrative. No tickets are sold. Dimensions and generated viewpoints are not measured venue data. Standing-area samples are unassigned. A clear-view preview hides decorative figures, not structural obstructions.

A short walkthrough

A recording of the working concept: overview and roof, a west-stand view, a south standing-area preview, and a locally saved viewpoint.

Download the silent demo video

Text alternative: The opening screen identifies the independent concept beside the animated stadium. The stadium explorer shows all four stands in overview, in daylight among trees, roads and buildings; the roof cutaway is switched off, closing the roof to its realistic opening, then on again to pull it back to a ring. Choosing the West stand card frames that stand from across the pitch. The view zooms in and the pointer hovers a generated seat, which shows a card to preview the view. Clicking it flies the camera to that illustrative viewpoint, just behind anonymous fans in the rows in front; the panel shows the seat with a €62 demo price, and the view turns left. Escape returns to the stadium view with the seat still under review. Change stand leads to the South stand, where a standing-area sample is chosen and previewed the same way. Setting People to 2 shows a €20 price and an illustrative €40 total, and Save viewpoint adds a saved-viewpoint card noting that no personal spot is assigned. Reset stadium returns to the overview, and the walkthrough scrolls back to the opening screen.

Original work & asset credits

The geometry, generated place data, interface, procedural surfaces, anonymous crowd, fictional teams, scoreboard and boundary displays are original project work created for this portfolio with Codex assistance. The animated stadium beside the opening headline is original too: a simplified diorama derived from the project’s own illustrative model and rendered live in the browser, with a still poster image rendered from the same model. The video records this application. No sponsor artwork, player likeness, reference photography, reference footage, music, or downloaded model is distributed. The explorer’s surface textures and sky come from generic public-domain material scans, listed below. None of them depicts the stadium or its surroundings.

The navigation uses the Borussia Dortmund crest supplied by the club’s public website. It is a trademark of Borussia Dortmund and appears only to identify the stadium subject within this clearly labeled independent concept.

The browser runtime uses React, React DOM, Scheduler, and Three.js under their MIT licenses, and the Basis Universal texture transcoder under the Apache License 2.0. Read the full third-party license notices. Original project work has no public reuse license granted.

Reference use & limits

User-supplied visual references and a curated stadium imagery pack informed architectural observation only. They are not included in this release. The repository's provenance records identify those inputs and their permitted uses. The four-stand form is a concept, not a surveyed reconstruction. Demo categories and prices do not describe an event or an official venue inventory.

Automated checks cover generated geometry, navigation, demo discovery, accessibility rules, fallback behavior and emulated mobile layouts. Safari/WebKit, manual screen-reader use, real devices, and real-world sightlines remain unverified. No accessibility certification or real-device performance claim is made.
